FAQs

Everything you need to know about joining our hackathon.

Do I need experience? +

No! This hackathon is open to all experience levels. Mentors and workshops will be available for every level to help you learn and build.

How do I join a team? +

Come solo or with friends! We’ll have team formation activities at the event, and you can also join the Discord beforehand to connect with others looking for a team.

Can I start my project early? +

No, projects must be started during the hackathon. Pre-existing projects are not allowed.

What is a hackathon? +

A hackathon is an event where students “hack” together creative solutions in 24–48 hours—like apps, chatbots, interactive dashboards, IoT devices, games, ML models, AI agents, or data science projects. There’s no malicious hacking involved.

What do I win? +

We’re offering $5K in prizes across our four main tracks: Sustainability, Civic Tech, Education, and Healthcare. More prize info coming soon.

Is it free? +

Yes! Participation is completely free. All hackers receive meals, swag, and access to workshops and mentors throughout the event.

Who can attend? +

This event is open to students of any college or university.

What activities will there be? +

Check the agenda for a mix of workshops, activities, and sponsor events to learn, take breaks, and network.

Where is the event? +

The hackathon is in person at the Duan Family Center for Computing & Data Sciences (665 Commonwealth Ave, Boston, MA).

Is parking available? +

Yes. There is free two-hour street parking, and Agganis Arena Lot A and Lot C2 offer public parking daily from 7 AM–11 PM.
Parking passes are not provided.

Do I have to stay overnight? +

Overnight stays are encouraged but not required. You may leave at night and return in the morning. Air mattresses and sleeping rooms will be available for all attendees.

Do I have to stay the whole time? +

No, you can come and go as needed, though staying for the full event will help you get the most out of the experience.

What should I bring? +

Bring your laptop, charger, and headphones, plus personal items like a toothbrush, deodorant, pillow/blanket, or sleeping bag. The building can get cold at night, so warm layers or blankets are recommended.

What is the team size limit? +

Teams can have up to 5 members. We’ll provide team-matching activities to help everyone find a group.

Is there a Code of Conduct? +

Yes. We follow the MLH Code of Conduct.

Still have a question? +
